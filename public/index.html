About Us

Powering Ghana's EV Revolution

EV Shop GH is Ghana's dedicated electric vehicle parts and sourcing company. We supply genuine EV components — batteries, motors, chargers, conversion kits — to technicians, businesses and individual owners across the country.

We also run a vehicle sourcing service, helping Ghanaians acquire electric cars, motorcycles and commercial vehicles from China, Europe and North America, with full documentation, shipping and customs clearance handled for you.

Our mission is simple: make electric mobility accessible and affordable for every Ghanaian.
